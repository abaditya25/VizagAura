      About VizagAura

      VizagAura delivers hyperlocal heat intelligence to residents of Greater Visakhapatnam. Built for the AWS Environmental Hackathon.

      Data sources

      Weather: Open-Meteo — free, no key
      Coordinates: OpenStreetMap Nominatim
      Municipal data: GVMC ward records (public domain)
      Risk bands: App-defined public-communication thresholds (see disclaimer)

      Indicative screening only. Not a medical assessment or official heat-health alert. Weather is a forecast-model estimate — not a local sensor reading. Always follow your local health authority.

      Coverage note
      Weather is available only for localities marked verified in the current dataset. Some entries remain unavailable while their coordinates or boundary status are reviewed. Forecast values are estimates for stored map points, not neighbourhood sensor readings.
